# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Hackathon judges and presenters using a single Google Pixel 7 in Chrome. They hold
the phone upright and turn their body to explore a place.

## Product Purpose

Open a fixed, street-level view outside Bastille Court and make the phone feel
like a window into that location. Success means a first-time user can enter the
experience with one tap and immediately understand that turning the phone changes
where they look. Story content will be layered onto the experience later.

## Brand Personality

Immediate, immersive, grounded.

## Anti-references

Avoid science-fiction AR styling, map-dashboard clutter, onboarding tours, and
controls that compete with the photosphere.

## Design Principles

- Put the place first and keep interface chrome secondary.
- Make the primary gesture obvious before the panorama opens.
- Design for one-handed use on a Pixel 7 in variable ambient light.
- Fail clearly when the panorama or WebGL is unavailable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use large touch targets, strong contrast, readable text, visible keyboard focus,
and touch-dragging as a fallback when motion tracking is unavailable or unwanted.
